# Useful-continuation follow-up

Fable 5.1 at xhigh recommended auditing the value of discarded continuations
before implementing another retention policy. The revised goal followed that
advice, while correcting unsupported statistical and terminal-validity inferences
in the proposed plan. The original 12-hour tranche remains complete and separate.

## Causal result

The prior Metroid probe's 156 discarded-only versus 42 survivor-only living exit
trials did not establish that retaining the discarded state would add useful
coverage. At equal total physical probe work, all extra maps reached only by
those discarded states were already on their source trajectories. **These samples
do not qualify a new retention policy.** This is a tested rejection of the
sampled archive-new-map hypothesis, not a proof of long-horizon equivalence or
an argument against all alternative retention.

The descriptive reanalysis first reproduced all frozen source/output hashes and
found 9/5/2 pair signs (discarded/survivor/tied). Among 260 trials where both states
survived, directional exit counts were 12/16. Conditioning on survival is not a
causal intervention; it motivated the equal-total-frame experiment.

F 02 gave each of 16 pairs' two sources exactly 100,000 physical suffix frames,
resampling ordinary 24-action suffixes after death or horizon completion. A fragile
source therefore received more attempts for the same emulator work. Two runs
produced identical summaries and outcome hashes.

| Equal-work map comparison | Pairs |
| --- | ---: |
| Discarded reaches additional maps | 4 |
| Survivor reaches additional maps | 2 |
| Identical reached-map sets | 10 |
| Discarded-exclusive map absent from known source paths | 0 |
| Survivor-exclusive map absent from known source paths | 1 |

The known-source union is only a lower bound on the campaign's prior observed
coverage. It is sufficient to reject novelty for the discarded-exclusive maps;
absence from it does not establish global novelty for the survivor. Full archive
membership and discovery histories were not retained. All 15 exported continuation
witnesses, including shared maps absent from known source coverage, passed two
independent ordinary-genesis replays. Their snapshot hashes match each other and
the producing probe snapshot; required living map visits occur after the
source-prefix boundary.
No equipment, capacity, or boss gains occurred.

## MM 2 transfer diagnosis

Neither saved fresh-chain campaign retains a complete archive. F 03 therefore
sampled its own Wily 1 champion trajectory using a fixed rule, with no outcome-based
replacement of starts. It found 2 frontier and 4 calibration sources in C 02, and 1
frontier and 4 calibration sources in C 03. The decoded stage origins match in all
reported fields, including 28 health,308 total weapon energy,3 lives, and all eight
weapons; full emulator-state equality is not claimed.

All 192 frontier trials produced living spatial extensions;8 ended dead. Many
visited cells absent from the champion trajectory, but none reached a higher
living non-scroll endpoint screen than 36 or defeated a boss. Calibration sources
also extended. This rejects simple physical inextendability of those sampled
frontier states. It does not establish forward progress, global novelty,
retention loss, or selection starvation. No unchanged fresh chain was rerun.

## Terminal evidence

The final panel contains 87 observed and eligible underflow action endpoints in
historical controls, versus 3271 observed and zero eligible underflow endpoints in
corrected searches. Different counts on diverged trajectories do not establish
false exclusions or explain depth regression. The existing mechanical diagnosis
remains bounded by its source evidence and tested examples. No terminal default
changes; the resource-plus-terminal interaction remains untested.
