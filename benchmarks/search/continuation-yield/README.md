The user requested consultation with **Fable 5.1 xhigh**, revision of the current
goal, and continued work. The consultation completed with the requested model;

## Decisions adopted

- Calibrate the frozen ordinary control before spending more on mechanism
  comparisons. The earlier one-seed percentage gates were allocation rules,
  not informative estimates of population effect or impossibility.
- Prefer testing selection-side allocation of useful continuations before a
  further retention descriptor. First check the existing selector's adaptation
  so the candidate is a distinct mechanism.
- Freeze an endpoint and a small multi-seed paired panel. Keep censoring and
  physical cost explicit. A replicated precursor improvement has value even
  when a boss or Wily 4 breakthrough remains unachieved.

## Corrections to the consultation

Fable overstates several claims. All locally missing map labels were seen
previously; that does not establish their current active availability, equal
capabilities, recovery cost or future behavior. Selected seven-competition
coverage gaps do not imply a small global retention effect ceiling. Observed
parent frequencies are empirical exposure under one adaptive history, not exact
sampling probabilities. A sample restricted to high-frequency slots and proxy
map labels is not a certified bound on adaptive search improvement. We will not
use the proposed S/Y thresholds as proofs or gates.

Its CV-based seed counts, 0.35/0.15 boundaries and max(0.15, 1.5 CV) effect gate
are uncalibrated heuristics. With censored milestone times, a success-only CV
would additionally be selection biased. We use restricted milestone cost and
hit indicators, interval bounds, explicit small-panel limitations and independent
confirmation instead. Fixed pilot gates are compute-allocation choices.

The advice's seven-day/60-emulator-hour allocation is not adopted. The renewed
tranche has an eight-hour deadline and explicit frame ceilings, with a short
first decision phase. No policy has yet qualified for a long campaign.

## First step

D01 audits compatible existing ordinary-control records, then runs no more than
four pre-listed fresh controls at a fixed 25M-frame budget if those records are
insufficient. Metroid's first missile-capacity acquisition is the fixed primary
endpoint. No candidate is selected from this calibration's favorable seeds.
All subsequent mechanism-screen seeds must be separately frozen.

## Completed calibration and selection decision

[D01's frozen registration](d01-registration.json) preceded all four new controls.
[Results](d01-results.json) and [interval analysis](d01-analysis.json) report four
attainments at 25M frames, with mean first-missile cost in
[18,167,834, 18,180,118.75] admitted frames. Individual arrival intervals span
14.086M–24.855M. The sample-CV interval is 0.2549–0.2559; it is descriptive,
not a confidence interval or power calculation. The 100M nominal allocation used
100,002,831 admitted frames including 2,831 frames of bounded in-flight drain.
All four stopped at the frame bound. Measured witness replay consumed 283615 additional
frames. Genesis/setup and unadmitted physical search work remain unmeasured.
No failed earlier retention comparison is reopened by these controls.

A [focused second Fable consultation](fable-selection-feedback.md), with
[recorded provenance](selection-consultation-provenance.json), recommends removing
selection-side historical cost ranks rather than adding a second yield estimator.
Existing barren counters already adapt to new-cell descendant yield. Entry-count
weighting is an existing, separate factor. The chosen ablation is specified in
[selection-hypothesis.md](selection-hypothesis.md).

The new selector has passed generic deterministic/replay/resource checks. Native
qualification must preserve the first D01 control's complete campaign stream and
exercise the candidate with full replay before a fresh paired panel. The panel
is not yet run and no performance gain is claimed.
