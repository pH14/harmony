## Decisions adopted

- Calibrate the frozen ordinary control before spending more on mechanism
  comparisons. The earlier one-seed percentage gates were allocation rules,
  not informative estimates of population effect or impossibility.
- Prefer testing selection-side allocation of useful continuations before a
  further retention descriptor. First check the existing selector's adaptation
  so the candidate is a distinct mechanism.
- Freeze an endpoint and a small multi-seed paired panel. Keep censoring and
  physical cost explicit. A replicated precursor improvement has value even
  when a boss or Wily 4 breakthrough remains unachieved.

## Corrections to the consultation

Fable overstates several claims. All locally missing map labels were seen
previously; that does not establish their current active availability, equal
capabilities, recovery cost or future behavior. Selected seven-competition
coverage gaps do not imply a small global retention effect ceiling. Observed
parent frequencies are empirical exposure under one adaptive history, not exact
sampling probabilities. A sample restricted to high-frequency slots and proxy
map labels is not a certified bound on adaptive search improvement. We will not
use the proposed S/Y thresholds as proofs or gates.

Its CV-based seed counts, 0.35/0.15 boundaries and max(0.15, 1.5 CV) effect gate
are uncalibrated heuristics. With censored milestone times, a success-only CV
would additionally be selection biased. We use restricted milestone cost and
hit indicators, interval bounds, explicit small-panel limitations and independent
confirmation instead. Fixed pilot gates are compute-allocation choices.

The advice's seven-day/60-emulator-hour allocation is not adopted. The renewed
tranche has an eight-hour deadline and explicit frame ceilings, with a short
first decision phase. No policy has yet qualified for a long campaign.
