# Conditional confirmation and transfer allocation

This allocation is written while S01 has one completed winning pair and the
second pair's control is still running. It does not change S01's registered
25M-frame endpoint, stopping rule or result. No follow-on experiment starts
unless the complete S01 screen passes.

The tranche has 500M nominal admitted search frames. D01 used 100M and a full
S01 uses 200M; their bounded drains are recorded separately. The remaining
200M can fund two four-pair panels without reducing the number of independent
seeds to one or two:

| Stage | Paired seeds | Per-arm cap | Maximum nominal admitted frames |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| R01, fresh Metroid confirmation | 4 | 20M | 160M |
| T01, fresh MM2 Metal transfer | 4 | 5M | 40M |

R01 retains first missile-capacity acquisition as its event and keeps the
qualified policy, binary, action/retention settings, workers, CPU placement and
archive capacity unchanged. Its restricted-cost estimand has a **20M** horizon,
not S01's 25M horizon. This is an independent check of the same event and
mechanism at a shorter fixed horizon; it must not be described as an independent
estimate of the 25M restricted mean. The horizon is chosen from the remaining
allocation, not from a search for favorable candidate results. It must pass the
same four-pair, three-strict-win, 15% interval-robust mean and resource criteria.
Unattained events remain censored at 20M. Failure stops this escalation path.

T01 uses a fresh standard Metal-stage start, with no earlier solution tape. Its
endpoint is first stage victory at a 5M-frame horizon, using the evaluator's
exact admitted-frame victory cost and twice-replayed witnesses. Existing Metal
records show completion at modest costs (for example 2.565M in J03 ordinary and
6.786M in the earlier ordinary C01), but differ in selector/configuration and
are neither matched controls nor a hit-rate estimate for T01. The short horizon
may censor both arms; such pairs remain ties, and cannot establish long-horizon
equivalence or lack of effect.

Before execution, freeze the exact ordinary MM2 action/suffix/mixture settings
from the existing workload evaluation, and use them in both arms. Compare the
qualified semantic cost selector with its no-cost ablation; do not compare the
candidate against a different semantic walk. The only within-game arm difference
is selection-side cost ranking. Use the same four-pair exploratory criteria as
S01 for a positive transfer claim. This evaluates transfer of the mechanism,
not whether its absolute performance beats every historical MM2 configuration.

Both stages need their own new seed labels, registrations, source/build/scorer
hashes, provenance checks and fixed resource caps before launch. Execution can
stop after completed pairs when the strict-win criterion becomes impossible.
No exploratory positive result is a population-level efficacy estimate. Even
if both stages pass, they establish a replicated precursor improvement rather
than the still-unachieved boss/Wily4 breakthrough. Longer campaigns and untouched
validation require their existing gates and enough remaining budget; no silent
extension or reassignment of the auxiliary allowance is permitted.
