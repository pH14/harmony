# RC01: one cheap conditional local-retry efficacy falsifier

RQ01 qualifies actual surviving retries without changing default artifacts.
CI01 shows that the ordinary archive already retained and revisited lower-HP
states, so an untested continuation change is more concrete than another claim
that it discarded all useful states. Neither observation proves that temporary
local retries improve combat. The finite-cap model has an adverse alive-trap
case; more surviving actions alone do not qualify the candidate.

Compare the qualified retry flag to default from the **original E01 HP140 root**,
with four prospectively fixed new paired seeds. Keep the same native source,
archive (512 MiB), selector, independent action draws and one-to-six command
attempt cap. Both arms receive 250k admitted attempted frames, at most5,000 jobs,
four workers, 4,096 actions and120 seconds search. A monotonic counter charges
failed attempts. Setup/unadmitted/reconstruction gaps remain unknown, so this
is a matched-admitted-work test, not exact total-physical-work equality.

The sole efficacy endpoint is replayed **Ridley defeat**. An event is in budget
only after execution zero and at an admitted observation no later than250k.
Record its interval from the previous admission through the reporting job;
require strict wins and the cost gate even at the adverse interval endpoints.
A no-hit result is complete only at the fixed frame horizon. Both-censored pairs
are ties at250k; execution/wall limits or failed replay are measurement failures.
No boss-HP minimum or number of retries can substitute for this endpoint.

The frozen registration specifies all seeds, request/source/build/asset hashes,
balanced sequential arm order on msr1 CPU0–3, a 35-minute complete-block deadline,
180-second process limits (210-second service hard stops), 2GiB process memory,
256MiB single-file and512MiB output limits. Verification helper work is capped
at2.5M physical frames per cell; all eight worst-case known cell costs fit the
separate25M auxiliary block. Full campaign/checkpoint and four witness replays
are compulsory. Any failure ends dispatch; no replacement or rerun.

A pass requires all four pairs, three strict wins, at least15% lower mean
restricted cost and aggregate candidate CPU/wall at most1.25 times control.
These are allocation rules, not a power calculation. After each pair, stop as
soon as three wins become impossible. The scorer and planted censoring, interval,
futility and resource counterexamples are frozen before execution.

A failed screen closes this retry candidate at this endpoint/horizon. Do not
rescue it with a new seed bank, damage endpoint, larger budget or selected root.
A pass only earns a new plan for fresh development and separately qualified
cross-host/MM2 transfer; it is never a fresh-search success or goal completion.
Use ms02 for that earned work rather than spend its time qualifying a candidate
before this cheaper conditional gate is resolved. All evidence stays in PR #287.
