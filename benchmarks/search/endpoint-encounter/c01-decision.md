# One bounded development census

Q01 qualifies the reporting path and default compatibility. The missing decision
is whether a depth-reaching campaign generates live endpoints with classified
boss context. Existing selected witnesses cannot answer it; another short fresh
precursor screen would spend work without reaching the relevant regime.

A zero-emulation audit of four already-selected development campaigns binds each
first-area report to its preceding/current progress records. Their first boss
areas were reported at approximately 147M, 196M, 295M and 360M admitted frames,
after approximately 21, 28, 43 and 45 minutes on ms02. These are selected examples,
not an incidence estimate or an expected-time distribution. Their different
policies are not pooled. The detailed intervals, source hashes and request
identities are in `deep-development-timing.json`.

Choose one reused seed, T03 corrected seed 5, because its recorded ordinary
control reached Ridley's area near 147M frames and Kraid's area near 159M. Run
from ordinary new-game genesis with the ordinary selector and independent action
law, not from its retained tape. Use the qualified audited binary on ms02, four
workers/8 GiB archive, 250M admitted frames, 35-minute search wall cap, three-minute
finish allowance, 12 GiB service memory and 4 GiB output cap. Exact identities,
source/build hashes and process limits are frozen in `c01-registration.json`.
The single 250M/4M auxiliary block is separate from the closed Q01/P01 blocks.
No second seed, longer horizon or changed policy is scheduled.

The new build includes motion metadata and versioned observation identities that
the historical v8 T03 build lacked. Ordinary group geometry, replacement and
input rules ignore that metadata, but this census is not claimed to reproduce
all historical T03 bytes or timing. Q01 supplies current-build physical/decision
compatibility at its short fixture. Retention auditing is disabled to avoid
unneeded sampling; its configuration is explicit. Compare no performance result
across these identities. Seed selection uses known development evidence and the
result is excluded from fresh validation even though execution starts at genesis.

A positive, twice-replayed producing endpoint earns standalone observer
qualification of that episode before a separate local-combat decision. A complete
negative describes all eligible admitted endpoints within this horizon, with
area entry reported separately. It still says nothing about unsampled interiors,
capability, absent future encounters or counterfactual retention. No boss-area
entry or an incomplete horizon ends the attempt without an automatic extension.
This experiment changes a previously unavailable fact; it is not another claim
that precursor gains imply a boss-level improvement. msr1 remains available for
independent episode qualification if the census yields one, rather than running
a slower duplicate of the same diagnosis.

The result reader `analyze_census.py` implements these frozen decisions without
emulation. It charges the new encounter replays in addition to ordinary witness
costs, requires the exact producing artifact and endpoint replay for a positive,
and refuses to turn an incomplete horizon or missing eligible samples into a
negative result. Four planted-evidence tests cover these boundaries; their
synthetic fixtures are not game-performance evidence. This reader was added
while the already-registered census was running and changes no allocation or gate.

At 64,325,902 admitted frames, a sparse live check found that Morph Ball, energy
tank, missile-capacity and Norfair discovery executions, route frames and saved
input hashes exactly match the selected historical T03 run. See
`c01-early-bridge.json`. This uses existing output and no new emulator work. It
supports relevance of the selected configuration through these four milestones;
it does not establish complete historical replay or guarantee later encounters.
