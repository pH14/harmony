# Prospectively test the exploratory persistence signal

D01 is closed with its registered component candidate rejected. Its matched
whole-command control reached the energy tank on both observed seeds, at
40,323,515 and 34,826,915 admitted frames; both ordinary controls were censored
at 50M. Their mean restricted-cost ratio is 0.7515043. These two observations
were selected after outcomes, so they nominate a hypothesis rather than satisfy
a registered confirmation gate. Do not pool them with P01.

P01 asks whether the **unchanged** whole-repeat policy improves this endpoint
on four independent fresh paired seeds. Keep the 37/210 repeat probability,
ordinary selector, retention, suffix durations, special taps, maximum trajectory
length, memory, frame horizon and native binaries fixed. No combination with
the earlier no-cost selector is tested. There are no new model parameters,
observer changes or emulator qualification costs.

This is a considered departure from the initial post-critique idea to build
encounter telemetry. The source audit found existing campaign-wide milestone
aggregation, and the proposed extra Ridley-area replay is already present in
NQ02. More counters or another replay would not resolve those negatives. In
contrast, the newly completed second persistence result makes an independent
test of that signal a concrete decision with a cheap futility boundary. The
adviser had only the first completed triplet, and did not endorse this new test.

The exact kernel proves a distinct temporal law and supplies adverse examples;
it supplies no game-utility guarantee. Temporally extended random exploration
has precedent in [Dabney, Ostrovski and Barreto](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.01782),
but their algorithm, duration law and experiments differ. The reason to spend
compute here is the two local observations plus an already qualified fixed
operator, not a claim that the paper predicts Metroid success.

The previous decision block closes below its ceilings at 647,118,811 admitted
search and 44,909,399 known auxiliary frames. P01 is a separately bounded next
block under the user's existing unrestricted research authorization, with a
maximum 400M nominal search frames plus bounded in-flight drain and 4M known
auxiliary frames. Prior budgets and results remain frozen; unused capacity is
not treated as evidence. Each cell has the existing 20-minute search limit,
180-second finish limit, 1,410-second outer limit, four CPUs, 8 GiB archive,
12 GiB process envelope and 4 GiB output cap. All seeds and host-local pairs
are registered before emulation. The whole panel expires at 02:50 UTC on
2026-09-10. No cell starts without its full time reservation.

Run the first two pairs on the two hosts concurrently, with opposite arm order.
Complete both arms of each pair. Stop further dispatch on measurement failure,
resource exhaustion, or when three strict wins in four become impossible.
Only an attainable gate earns the second two registered pairs; do not replace
seeds or extend a horizon. Passing needs three strict wins, at least 15% lower
mean restricted milestone cost and aggregate candidate CPU/elapsed totals at
most 1.25 times control. Both-censored pairs tie. These are allocation rules,
not power or statistical-significance claims.

A failure closes this persistence proposal without tuning or another precursor
panel. A pass establishes replicated evidence only at the energy-tank endpoint.
It earns a bounded same-policy MM2 transfer and later-depth decision, requiring
a separately frozen useful endpoint, matched controls and affordable complete
panels. It does not establish an encounter or combat bottleneck and does not
earn untouched boss/Wily validation. The original full objective remains unmet.
