## Corrections and limits

The two suspected implementation gaps are resolved by source inspection. Both
samplers choose the ordinary duration from fixed bounds without branching on
the selected direction/A-B command. This is independence under the stated
ideal-random-draw model, not a PRNG independence proof. Both adapters propagate
`apply` errors with `?`; there is no silent independent-suffix fallback.

A final archive can reveal currently cached/restorable states, with missing
snapshots explicitly unavailable. An empty final census cannot show that a
campaign never generated or retained an encounter state. A selected input or
an origin checkpoint is not a whole archive. An active boss classification,
positive health and ammunition do not by themselves prove the state is
fight-capable; "loadable" is not a qualified predicate. Name raw conditions
precisely before turning them into endpoints.

One observed HP decrease would falsify a claim of no such local continuation
from that tested state. It would not establish that selection is the only
bottleneck, that defeat is reachable at the tested budget, or that another
resource/retention policy is warranted. Zero decreases in a bounded probe
would not prove universal action-law inadequacy. Unavailable comparisons remain
distinct from zero. NQ02 exercised real native restores on a non-boss route;
positive boss classification came from the FCEUX reference/model checks, not a
positive native fight. No unqualified native census reader should be assumed.

The ordinary calibration's 3/4 hits are not a population hit probability or a
power calculation. Zero observed control victories also do not make every
comparative experiment incapable of detecting improvement. They make another
long, weakly motivated comparison unattractive. A replicated precursor gain
would remain useful evidence at that endpoint, while leaving the full goal
unproved. The adviser received only the then-complete triplet. The whole-command
control subsequently finished with two exploratory wins. Neither the literature
nor these two results establishes a successful confirmed policy; preserve them
as the motivation for a separate prospective test, not a rescue of D01.
