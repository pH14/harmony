## 1. Audit of the retained-prefix opportunity argument

**Verdict: sound as a conditional, model-based opportunity bound. No fatal error. One expository slip, one load-bearing premise, several unstated software premises.**

Checks I recomputed:

- Per-draw step. Conditional on tape/history/parent, P(N≤L)=L/6 gives Y=1, W=0; otherwise W≤120L. The rational upper bounds 1, 427/432, 23/24, 101/112, 29/36, 31/48, 3/8 for L=0…6 are all correct, and e^{L/10}≤10/(10−L) is valid (geometric-series bound, L<10). Actual values 1.000, 0.982, 0.937, 0.859, 0.743, 0.581, 0.368.
- Expository error: e^{-1}≤3/8 needs the exponential series through the x⁴ term (five terms). The first four terms give 1/3, which is a *lower* bound. Harmless, but the text is wrong as written.
- Load-bearing premise at L=1. The inequality holds only if P(N≤1) ≥ (e^{0.1}−1)/(e^{0.1}−e^{−1}) ≈ 0.143. Uniform{1..6} gives 0.167 — a margin of 2.4 points. Any length law with lighter weight on N=1 breaks the constant, and the script's exact-rational check assumes uniform. "Length drawn first" is not "length uniform"; verify the exact length law in the registered `alphabet_only` configs.
- Supermartingale/Ville. Valid in the enriched filtration containing the latent six-action tape; adaptive parent choice, cap truncation (conservative, as claimed), endpoint/futility stopping (Ville is time-uniform) and stopping before the guard are all handled. Covering the two unrun trajectories in the union is wasteful but valid. Arithmetic: 1200·(1401+6·ln160)=1,717,741 ✓; 0.170% ✓. Draw accounting closes exactly (8,037,314+1,401=8,038,715), so there is no unmodeled third draw outcome — good.
- Unstated software premises (not errors, but the bound depends on them): (i) no ordinary action in those exact control configs exceeds 120 frames; (ii) the skip predicate is exactly "all N prefix inputs currently retained from this parent," the same owner notion a shortcut would restore; (iii) the skip counter counts nothing else (over-inclusion is conservative, under-inclusion is not); (iv) parent chosen before N, N before actions, cap applied after; (v) L is evaluated at dispatch under concurrent workers, same as the hypothetical restore.

Interpretation: 0.17% is a model quantity about frames under free restore, with model probability ≥0.9625. It is not native confidence. But the allocation conclusion is right for a reason stronger than the bound: emulation is deterministic, so restoring a retained prefix reproduces the identical state. Such a shortcut cannot change what is discovered — only cost and concurrency timing. The bound says nothing about unretained failed repetitions (#283), nothing about hash-equivalent states reached by different inputs, and neither funds nor closes attempt-exposure measurement. Its "decision" should be read as closing the shortcut only.

## 2. One direction: fight-progress retention (target damage as an archive-key coordinate)

**The evidence pattern the record already contains.** Inside the encounter, damage happens (140→139→131→130→129; 7/32 surviving-damage cases under full resources) but never compounds: every episode dies; the HP129 state received four jobs with zero retention candidates; the 31 inactive states have no HP<140. The archive key is spatial/named-progress; boss HP is deliberately kept out of keys and rewards. So inside the boss room the search is progress-blind: a child at HP128 that lands in an already-occupied cell is an unretained duplicate, exactly like a child that dealt no damage. The six damaged states were retained only because they were otherwise novel. This is a retention-criterion failure, not a selection, continuation, retry, cutoff, oracle, or resource failure — none of the closed nominees tested it.

**Mechanism.** Add a bucketed target-HP coordinate to the retention key while a same-boundary boss classification is active. Retained damage states become parents; damage accumulates over chained jobs the same way spatial novelty accumulates elsewhere. This is the same kind of read as the existing named milestones (Bombs, missiles), applied to the terminal objective; MM2's Wily-machine HP is the symmetric hook, so the hypothesis is generic in mechanism with one per-game address, and both-game evaluation stays available.

**Offline decision first (zero emulation).** The summary says endpoint observer coverage is complete at action boundaries and that bounded retention-competition inputs from the 250k snapshot-root pilot survive. Query: among all executed action endpoints in that pilot — retained or rejected — how many are classified with boss HP below their parent's HP, and how many of those were rejected as key duplicates? Three outcomes, each decisive:
- Rejected damage endpoints exist (HP below the retained minimum, or below parent) → key-blindness is discarding fight progress; the native pair below is earned.
- Damage endpoints exist only among retained states, and rejected children never dealt damage → the bottleneck is the action law (random ≤120-frame suffixes rarely fire usefully), not the key; do not run the pair. The next question becomes action structure, not retention, and I would want that stated as a separate hypothesis before anything runs.
- The artifacts cannot distinguish these (observer covers cached states only) → the pair below must record endpoint HP for every executed job in both arms; that is one counter, not a framework.

**Cheapest native falsifier (only after outcome one).** Same snapshot root as the existing pilot, no root selection, no duration or parameter sweep. Two arms, identical binary except the registered key variant: control = unchanged key; candidate = key ∪ floor(bossHP/10), active only while the boss classification is positive. Three preregistered seeds, ≤25M frames/15 min per cell, six cells ≈150M frames, ms02 only. Primary outcome: minimum classified boss HP among retained live states at cap, strictly lower wins. Secondary: defeat (HP=0, Samus alive, same-boundary read). Tertiary: count of executed damage endpoints per arm, to confirm the mechanism rather than luck. Futility rule as in CC01: the first pair the candidate fails to win closes the direction; no seed replacement, no horizon extension.

**Stopping rule.** Close if: the offline query returns outcome two; or any pair is lost; or all three pairs are won but the candidate's HP-decrease rate per frame is not larger than the control's damage-endpoint rate (that would mean retention is not compounding, only sampling). A snapshot-root win earns exactly one thing: a registered fresh-search integration test with the key active only after boss-room entry, on the existing Ridley-reaching genesis line, plus the MM2 Wily-boss analogue. It does not earn untouched validation, and it does not earn #285.

**Honest uncertainty.** This is a snapshot-local hypothesis. Even a defeat at the root would leave the fresh objective unproved: the only fresh encounter arrived at 7.9 energy and zero missiles, and fresh search reaches Ridley rarely. If fight-progress retention works locally but fresh encounters remain depleted, #285 becomes the next question — and only then would prior evidence fund it. If the offline query returns outcome two, the direction dies in an afternoon with no frames spent, which is the point of doing it first.

## 3. Closure rules broader than their registered failures require

Preserve as written: CC01 futility with no pooling, seed/horizon/endpoint rescue or bank/order pivot; no MM2 transfer for the continuation candidate; no reconstruction of deep campaigns to fill unclear gaps; no fourth control or sweep for the action study; the unrun CC01 pairs stay unrun.

Three rules are broader than their registered claims:

1. **"No further selected root or duration/parameter sweep is earned."** The registered failures are the local-retry candidate (censored ties) and the single-root controller/oracle/counterfactual line (no defeat). Both were mechanisms tested *at* the root, and both are closed. The root itself is not a failed claim; it is the only encounter-bearing test bed you have. Read narrowly: no root hunting, no sweeps. It should not forbid a distinct mechanism at the existing root with its own preregistered falsifier.

2. **"Reports do not guide search, keys or rewards."** As a measurement-integrity rule this is correct and should stay: observer output must never leak into the arm it measures. But it is being carried as a closure rule, and it forecloses the one hypothesis the encounter evidence points at. The distinction: a registered policy variant that reads the same RAM as a key coordinate, pinned as a separate identity and measured by the unchanged observer, is a policy under test, not a report guiding search. Keep the separation; drop the implicit prohibition.

3. **"Retained-prefix reuse does not earn the next experiment."** True of the shortcut. The document's own limits section says the bound does not cover unretained repetition or first-exposure benefit. The rule should be scoped to "does not fund a retained-prefix shortcut or duplicate filter," so it does not read as closing #283.

What is *not* a genuinely new hypothesis: re-running the continuation candidate with different banks, extending the retry horizon, choosing a better-resourced root, or sweeping suffix durations at the root. What *is* new: no gate has ever tested whether the retention criterion can see fight progress. The offline query in §2 costs nothing and tells you whether that hypothesis deserves 150M frames or should be closed immediately.
