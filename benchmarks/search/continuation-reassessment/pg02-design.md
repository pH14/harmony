# PG02 decision: test conditional living boss defeat at measured cost

PM01 passes the accounting prerequisite. The next useful test is whether the
unchanged PG01 progress rule compounds into living named boss defeats, beyond
ordinary retention and its scoped capacity control. More damage, admissions or
retained states are not the endpoint. This document is a prospective design,
**not a native allocation or seed registration**.

Use the same qualified, normally reconstructed encounter and feature build in
all arms. Keep the policy, exact projection, alphabet-only action law, selector,
four workers, one result slot, 512 MiB archive and terminal semantics fixed. Freeze
four new conditional seeds before running; neither PG01's seed nor any earlier
failed panel is reused as an efficacy sample. Compare three arms per seed:
ordinary, `resource_guarded_progress_2_v1`, and
`resource_guarded_progress_quality_control_2_v1`. The latter shares eligibility,
so a candidate win isolates its higher-progress ranking from that capacity rule.

The proposed horizon is 1M admitted search frames plus the fixed 4,645 constructor
frames: B = 1,004,645 physical search frames, with at most 2,880 drain frames recorded
separately. Raising the caller's execution safety ceiling from 5,000 to 20,000 is a
required bounded preflight change; otherwise an execution stop could censor the
frame comparison early. Keep a short search watchdog and at most 180 seconds per
whole cell, including full replay and witness checks. Register every CPU, memory,
output, frame, execution and outer wall limit before any run. At most two cells
may overlap on disjoint four-CPU sets. Freeze their order and all seeds before
observing results. No throughput or pilot retuning is part of this screen.

A valid cell must finish its frame horizon or produce a replayed living named
defeat within the admitted budget. The meter must close every target and verify
search physical work equals admitted work plus 4,645 constructor frames; full replay
has its separate 929-frame setup. Complete campaign/checkpoint and living-witness
checks are mandatory. A wall/execution cutoff before the horizon, accounting
failure, missing output or replay mismatch invalidates the allocation and stops
it without retry; it is not a shorter censored success or zero-cost failure.

For an attaining cell, score min(P,B), where P is all measured search-engine work
through the final drained stop. For nonattainment at the fixed horizon, score B.
This charges actual work through completion, rather than estimating the unknown
physical concurrency state at the first observation. It also avoids a success-only
mean. Keep the full actual drain, replay and direct-helper costs alongside that
restricted score. Late attainment whose final cost exceeds B has score B and
cannot manufacture a strict win against censored control.

To advance, the fixed candidate must have at least three strict wins in four pairs
and at least 15% lower mean restricted physical cost **against each comparator**.
Both-censored pairs are ties. These are allocation rules, not confidence or power
claims. Evaluate futility after complete seed triples: if three wins against either
comparator are unattainable, leave the remaining triples unrun. A failed candidate
does not automatically promote the capacity control or earn a longer horizon.
A pass earns a separate fresh-development decision; it is not fresh-search success.

Before launch, publish one concrete registration binding the new caller build,
requests, seed list, ordering, source/input identities, physical-work ledger and
fixed scorer. Budget full search, constructors, replay, witnesses and worst-case
drain once. PM01 is closed and cannot fund this work. Independent confirmation,
MM2 transfer and untouched fresh validation remain later requirements of the full
goal; do not pool this conditional screen with those panels.
