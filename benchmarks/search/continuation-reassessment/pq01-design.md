# PQ01: use the saved state distribution to choose the next question

PG02 is invalid and closed; no completion or retry is proposed. Its two completed
binary calls nevertheless leave exact replay-verified checkpoints and parent
selection streams. Before changing retention or selection, ask whether the fixed
progress rule's saved lower-HP states were subsequently selected, and what player
resources accompany those states. Neither 445 alternate admissions nor a selected
champion answers that question.

The stream records retained IDs and parent choices, but not raw HP. The existing
checkpoint inspector can export IDs and mechanical state without an emulator.
Reading classified HP uses its existing paused-context getter: one ordinary
929-frame constructor, then exact restores with zero subsequent frame changes.
This is a proposed bounded state read, not another policy or efficacy trial.
No native allocation exists until a separate registration binds the requests.

First inventory both saved PG02 checkpoints with the typed decoder locally.
Their 38,504,277/37,229,259 byte sizes require the inspector's bounded input ceiling
to increase from 32 to 64 MiB and its entry ceiling from 1,000 to 2,048. No search,
retention or projection code changes. Check unique IDs and the matching origin.

If the inventory qualifies, freeze both original checkpoints, root, native core,
ROM, source/build and two inspector calls. A separate allowance of at most 2,000
physical frames covers exactly two 929-frame constructions. Every paused restore
must preserve the complete snapshot and physical clock; no action, search or
continuation calls are permitted. Use one CPU, at most 1 GiB per service, 45 seconds
per child and 120 seconds for the whole two-read service, with bounded input/output.
Retain success and failure records before service collection; the wrapper's
successful reporting exit must not imply successful scientific checks.

Report classified cached-state HP and player-resource distributions, plus actual
parent selections for those immutable IDs since their creation. Distinguish HP255,
unclassified context, defeated flags, and states not selected after creation.
Observe both arms in full; do not select a new root or a favorable subset to test.

Checkpoint membership alone does not encode final active membership. Reconstruction
anchors can stay cached after replacement. Unless a source-backed inference can
be checked for a particular arm, label the population cached states, not active
representatives. Selection counts prove actual visits to a saved state; they do
not establish missed opportunities during its lifetime or an unbiased comparison
of states discarded earlier. Grouping by the final snapshot conditions on survival.
Snapshot HP differences are not lifetime damage or guaranteed recoverability.

This query can distinguish two different next questions: whether visibly damaged
saved states received further trials, and whether the observed resource/progress
tradeoff makes simple progress ranking inadequate. It cannot by itself establish
which policy would win, justify longer horizons, or promote a candidate. Choose a
new mechanism-level prediction only after this evidence is available. Earlier
failed gates, independent confirmation, MM2 transfer and untouched validation
requirements remain unchanged. Use ms02 only; msr1 is unavailable.
