# PC01: does the actual discarded state offer a useful future?

RR02 establishes 22 local losses of partial boss progress, all resource-equal.
It does not establish useful continuation loss. Export the already frozen first
record, 692/execution423/incumbent86, without emulation. Verify all3770 capture
records through the footer, stream/origin identity, and both snapshot/input hashes.
`pc01-pair/manifest.json` records the successful zero-frame export. The local
inputs are checkpoint-relative; neither is a boot-replay witness.

Let sC and sI be those complete candidate/incumbent states. A shared command
sequence u defines two deterministic trajectories F(sC,u) and F(sI,u). This is
a conditional comparison of actual states; equal resources do not isolate HP
from player pose, enemy phase or projectile differences. One counterexample can
refute blanket dominance of sI over sC for the measured utility. It cannot show
average dominance, sufficient archive representation, fresh-search improvement
or independence among tails. A negative finite bank cannot prove dominance.

The fixed bank has32 ordinary `sample_chord` tails,128 commands each. Seed i is
the first8 bytes, little-endian, of SHA256 of UTF-8
`harmony:pc01:record692:tail:{i}` for i=0..31. Seeds and complete generated commands
are frozen before native execution; there is no selection by observed outcome.
Each arm has the same command budget and8192-frame ceiling. Omit a whole command
that would exceed the ceiling. Stop on corrected BCD-underflow death, area exit,
living named boss defeat at a complete command, or ending. Retain partial terminal
commands as incomplete; they cannot supply a common completed comparison. Run
both arms for all32 tails unless measurement/resource failure stops the allocation.
Alternate arm order by tail index. Do not stop the bank on a scientific positive.

Primary descriptive outcome is a living named Ridley defeat at a completed
command within the fixed horizon. Report both arms' outcomes including death,
nonattainment and unequal actual exposure from terminal stopping. The secondary
counterexample compares common completed command boundaries with identical
elapsed physical frames, both alive. Require an uninterrupted, qualified same-boss
HP interval from each freshly restored baseline, **strictly more new HP loss**,
strictly lower remaining bossHP, and no worse current health, missiles, capacities,
equipment or boss flags. Count both directional witnesses and preserve every
tail. An inherited137-versus140 baseline with no new damage is not a witness.
Missing classification, HP increase, identity change or unexplained loss makes
that root interval permanently unavailable; later reappearance does not repair it.
Visible identity cannot rule out invisible same-key reloads, so these remain
observed HP losses, not certified lifetime damage.

Every restore must preserve the complete snapshot, context and physical clock.
Every measured command boundary, including clipped terminal commands, is checked
by a second held-command replay from the same exact snapshot: emulator bytes,
mechanical state, boss context and terminal state must agree. Diagnostic sampling
and verification costs are separate. Snapshot metadata representing the most
recent action's observation history is not compared between different chunkings.
All input hashes and the snapshot's embedded core identity are checked before
constructing the emulator. No resource/HP edit, foreign-state identity rewrite,
fresh campaign or policy modification is involved.

A candidate-only surviving defeat or a candidate secondary witness earns a
further retention-design decision, **not** an automatic policy experiment. If
neither occurs, close this pair/bank without rescue. Both-way witnesses indicate
a tradeoff and must not be simplified to scalar-HP dominance. Any later candidate
still needs a minimal source falsifier, separately registered matched development,
independent confirmation and transfer before untouched fresh validation. Existing
failed gates remain closed and the original boss/Wily goal remains unachieved.
