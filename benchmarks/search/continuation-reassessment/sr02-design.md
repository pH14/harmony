## Event intervals, success and futility

The event is recorded at ordered job admission. For a hit, the scorer finds that
job in the fully replayed stream and verifies that its cumulative frames equal
the first-event receipt. Its cost interval is the admitted total immediately
before that job through the total after it, plus the fixed 4,645 search-constructor
frames. This deliberately leaves the unobserved within-job event position
uncertain. A censored cell has the exact restricted interval
[1,004,645, 1,004,645]. Already reserved drain is charged in complete physical work;
it cannot pretend to be earlier discovery. Root-inherited, dead, late and wrong
boss events fail the frozen cell gate.

A pass requires all four quartets and, against **each** of the three comparators:

- at least three strict wins, defined as candidate upper cost < comparator lower;
- sum of candidate upper costs <= 85% of comparator lower costs;
- total candidate whole-child CPU microseconds and summed child peak RSS each
  <= 125% of the corresponding comparator totals, plus every hard resource gate.

These exact integer allocation rules are not power calculations, confidence
bounds or campaign-wide stochastic-dominance claims. The resource comparison
includes the child process's direct helpers and full replay. Both-censored pairs
are ties. Stop for futility only when a comparator's strict wins plus remaining
quartets cannot reach three. Do not stop early because a ratio looks favorable
or unfavorable. An invalid cell stops further launches and leaves the screen
invalid; every launched component remains in the cost ledger. A control is never
promoted if the nominated candidate fails.
