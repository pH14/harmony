# Fresh continuation comparison at first Bombs

Test the unchanged `alphabet_continuation_v1` against `alphabet_only`, with the
ordinary semantic-cost selector at cutoff3 and all other policies fixed. The
candidate reuses observed exit tails after a same-slot preference improvement,
on at most one reservation in four. It introduces no game route or externally
supplied action sequence. The existing bank, queue order and suffix bounds stay
unchanged. Equal seed labels do not imply identical draws once the policies'
adaptive dispatch diverges.

The completed historical audit finds three interval-robust Bombs wins and about
43% lower mean admitted cost, despite about43% worse energy-tank cost. FW02
establishes that all six original witnesses remain compatible with corrected
terminal rules; CQ01 establishes actual current continuation dispatch and full
replay on both architectures. These are nomination and qualification evidence,
not a fresh efficacy result. Terminal and key changes also make the existing
corrected seed5 control reach Bombs at113.33M rather than263.2M frames; the old
candidate effect cannot be carried forward.

The plausible mechanism is greater useful reuse after transitions accumulate.
It can fail when a tail learned from one state does not transfer to its
replacement, or when repeated familiar transitions displace better exploration.
The historical early regressions and lost later discoveries are adverse
evidence. Current source fixtures also expose label-dependent queue order;
fixing that here would change the nominee and confound this comparison.

Select first Bombs before fresh execution. Early milestones remain secondary,
not surrogate gates; an energy-tank gain or damage observation cannot rescue a
failed Bombs comparison. A fixed200M horizon lies above the observed corrected
control arrival and within the historical candidate's88.8–263.3M range. This
does not claim coverage of the current arrival distribution or statistical
power. Both-censored pairs are ties, not a success-only exclusion. Do not extend
the horizon after seeing results.

Use four fresh seed pairs, sampled after CQ01 and checked against212 local
tracked summary/result/registration/seed files and1,892 saved ms02 records.
Neither audit finds a match or skip. msr1 was reassigned and is unavailable;
copied records are included locally, while unrecorded historical use cannot be
certified. These seeds are development data, not untouched boss/Wily validation.

ms02's cores0–7 are distinct cores with the same reported5.5GHz maximum. Split
them into0–3 and4–7, each with sequential arms and one control-first/one
candidate-first pair over the complete panel. Recent50M ms02 cells took342–357
seconds; a200M horizon with a45-minute search cap is a feasibility extrapolation,
not a promise. Each process also has3M executions,4,096 actions,8GiB archive,
12GiB service memory,4GiB output,180 finishing seconds and a2,910-second outer
limit. A pair's5,900-second service owns its complete process group. Two worst
case waves fit within four hours, followed by an hour reserved for synthesis.

Initially run only pairs0/1. Each started pair is a bounded two-arm unit; its
own measurement/resource failure stops that unit. Complete the started wave
and score it before dispatching anything else. Zero wins after two pairs makes
three wins impossible and closes the panel. Any incomplete measurement or
resource overrun also disqualifies it; never restart a failed process.

A pass needs at least three strict wins, at least15% lower mean restricted
admitted Bombs cost, and candidate/control event-stopped CPU and wall ratios
at most1.25. These are allocation rules, not population significance. Admitted
work includes each job's origin reconstruction; setup, final witness replay
and unavailable failure-boundary work retain their separate accounting. The
separate block caps nominal search at1.6B plus recorded bounded drain and known
auxiliary work at32M; preserve actual spent work even if a limit fails.

The ordinary six-action drain estimate does not bound this candidate:
`ContinuationBank::ACTION_CAP` is128 and `OneToSix::bound_time` leaves learned
tails intact. Moreover, a job may reconstruct its parent from a keyframe. Use
the conservative whole-input bound for both arms: origin replay is at most the
parent's action count, and the suffix respects the remaining4,096-action cap.
The current longest hold is120 frames; use a conservative128-frame envelope.
Eight outstanding jobs therefore cost at
most8×4,096×128 =4,194,304 frames beyond the200M reservation cutoff. Record actual
drain separately; this bound grants no improvement credit beyond200M and does
not alter the nominal scoring horizon. CQ01 stopped at2,000 executions below
its frame cap, so its compatibility result was not a test of this drain bound.

Only a pass earns separately frozen independent confirmation at the unchanged
endpoint, horizon and policy, followed by MM2 transfer. Untouched boss/Wily

closes this screen without a longer horizon, replacement seed, earlier-endpoint
rescue, bank/order variant or automatic heuristic pivot. Keep all work in PR287.
