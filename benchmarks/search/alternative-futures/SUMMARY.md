**No breakthrough was achieved.** The complete untouched Metroid panel has
**0/10 boss successes for the terminal correction and 0/10 for control**. Both
fresh MM2 fixed-order chains obtained all eight weapons and stopped at Wily 1;
no MM2 candidate qualified the five-seed untouched panel.

## What the experiments establish

Discarded archive states can contain locally useful futures that their survivors
miss under shared sampled suffixes. Preserving two resource extremes did not
turn that counterexample into deeper fresh search under the tested conditions.
The strongest separate diagnosis is a Metroid terminal-classification defect:
a transient BCD health underflow can be admitted as an excellent living endpoint,
yet the inspected endpoint becomes zero-health on the next frame under all
256 controller masks. A versioned correction excludes it. Mechanical correctness
did not produce better held-out depth: secondary outcomes were predominantly
worse or mixed. The value of this tranche is reproducible causal evidence and
bounded negative interventions, rather than a claimed depth improvement.

Trials within one pair share source states and are correlated. The paired probe
counts are descriptive evidence, not independent seed successes. Area exits are
local extendability evidence, not proof of globally useful behavior.

Selection counters are censuses at removal. An intermediate state can already
have outgoing actions in its birth suffix, and pending jobs receive parent credit
only after ordered admission. Thus zero admitted parent-job credit does not mean
zero physical exploration. The age histogram does not resolve this ambiguity.
The resource-retention campaigns used historical terminal semantics. Their
negative results do not rule out resource retention combined with the corrected
terminal policy; that interaction has not received a matched campaign ablation.
See #283 for the separate measurement needed before a stronger starvation claim;
#281 tracks the missing distinction between boss-area entry and actual encounters.

## Untouched Metroid validation

The candidate, ten unused seeds, comparison rules, and resources were frozen
before inspection. Each arm uses four workers, an 8 GiB logical budget,
3M executions / 400M admitted frames, and 4096 actions. Arms differ only in
`death_or_ending_v2` versus `death_or_bcd_underflow_or_ending_v3`. All 20 cells
completed the 3M-execution anchor and verified exported named witnesses twice
from ordinary genesis on independent targets. Neither arm defeated a boss.

At each pair's common admitted-frame boundary, corrected/control attainment is
9/10 versus 10/10 for Bombs, 8/10 versus 10/10 for Kraid area, and 2/10 versus
4/10 for Ridley area. Long Beam instead improves from 2/10 to 6/10. These are
secondary observations; area entry does not establish a boss encounter.
Observed coverage is higher under correction on three seeds and lower on seven.
Maximum observed capacity is higher on two, equal on two, and lower on six;
maximum tank count is lower on three and equal on seven. These campaign-wide
maxima need not coexist on one trajectory.

## Fresh MM2 transfer and development boundaries

The two completed fresh MM2 chains used Metal, Heat, Air, Wood, Bubble, Quick,
Flash, Crash, then Wily stages. Each carried only newly searched victories from
its own chain and verified adapter transitions. The representative chain spent
286,628,099 admitted frames; the resource chain spent 323,395,219. Both obtained
all eight weapons and failed at Wily 1 under their registered wall limits.
The initial chain-export failure and its spent work remain preserved. These are
fresh fixed-order chains, not unrestricted whole-game search or untouched-panel
successes. No historical solution tape was used as a fresh starting state.

At matched 50M-frame local Wily 1 work, the tested resource, first-exposure,
duration, no-splice, and cell-cost variants did not recover depth. The no-splice
arm increased ordinary attempts about 2.64 times without resolving the bottleneck.
A raw follow-up rejects immediate terminality of the inspected champion: it
remains alive through 120 neutral frames during an automatic camera transition.
Screen 36 is a visited watermark, not a stable frontier or boss-reach surrogate.

Metroid development and local diagnostics remain distinct from validation.
The corrected development anchors improved late discovery on seeds 3 and 5 and
regressed on 4; all had zero bosses. Middle-duration sampling improved short-run
coverage on all three development seeds but traded late capabilities at the long
anchor. The longer development-discovered Kraid-area probe still produced no boss
at 100M admitted frames. These results do not justify a larger unchanged budget.
