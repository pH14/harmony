`witness.mp4` is an emulator recording of that unmodified game — its graphics
and its music are Damian Yerrick's GPL-licensed work — driven by a
search-generated controller tape, followed by neutral input. It is therefore

[GPL-3.0-or-later](https://www.gnu.org/licenses/gpl-3.0.html), including any
Harmony contribution to it; underlying works retain their original notices.
Preserve this notice with redistributed video. This media licence does not
change the licence of Harmony's code or benchmark reports.

The film contains the complete retained controller tape and a 180-frame neutral
tail. `run-summary.json` records the seed, execution budget, the champion's
defence evidence, and whether the town was lost. An unsolved campaign can show
a town that loses buildings, and the neutral tail shows what the town does with
no further input.

The campaign uses the separately source-built QuickNES libretro core pinned by
Harmony in `scripts/build-quicknes-core.sh`. The generated ROM, its debug
symbols, and the emulator library are build intermediates and are not part of
the uploaded CI artifact. The reproducible recipes are
`workloads/nes/scripts/build-thwaite-rom.sh`, with inputs pinned in
`workloads/nes/thwaite-versions.env`, and `scripts/build-quicknes-core.sh`.
